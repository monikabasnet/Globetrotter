Top Attractions in Nepal

Adventure

Mount Everest Base Camp

Trek to the base of the world's highest peak at 8,849m. Experience breathtaking Himalayan vistas, Sherpa culture, and the legendary Khumbu region.

Learn More →
Heritage

Kathmandu Durbar Square

The historic heart of Kathmandu featuring ancient palaces, temples, and courtyards. Home to Hanuman Dhoka Palace and the residence of Kumari, the Living Goddess.

Learn More →
Culture

Kumari - The Living Goddess

Witness the sacred Living Goddess tradition at Kumari Ghar. A young girl worshipped as the divine incarnation of Taleju Bhawani, appearing at her window for devotees.

Learn More →
Adventure

Annapurna Circuit

One of the world's greatest treks circling the Annapurna massif through diverse landscapes—from subtropical forests to high alpine deserts at Thorong La Pass (5,416m).

Learn More →
Hidden Gem

Rara Lake

Nepal's largest lake in remote western Nepal, surrounded by pristine forests and snow-capped peaks. Crystal-clear waters reflect the sky in this untouched wilderness paradise.

Learn More →
Spiritual

Swayambhunath Stupa

Ancient Buddhist stupa atop a hill overlooking Kathmandu Valley, adorned with the all-seeing Buddha eyes. A sacred pilgrimage site dating back over 2,000 years.

Learn More →
Hidden Gem

Gosaikunda Lakes

Sacred alpine lakes at 4,380m in Langtang National Park. Hindu pilgrims gather during Janai Purnima festival, believing Shiva created these holy waters with his trident.

Learn More →
Scenic

Poon Hill Sunrise

Famous sunrise viewpoint at 3,210m offering spectacular 360° panoramas of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges. Short accessible trek perfect for witnessing Himalayan dawn magic.

Learn More →
Hidden Gem

Khaptad National Park

Remote far-western Nepal sanctuary with rolling meadows, pristine forests, and spiritual hermitage sites. Named after revered sage Khaptad Baba who meditated here for decades.
